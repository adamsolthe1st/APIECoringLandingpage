# DESIGN.md
Project: Coring Services Landing Page
Owner: Apie Engineering
Objective: Build a conversion-focused landing page that attracts leads via ads and direct search, emphasizes affordability and precision, and drives WhatsApp inquiries.
Goals
- Present professional coring services clearly and persuasively.
- Convert visitors into leads via WhatsApp CTA.
- Showcase pricing tiers while emphasizing affordability.
- Build credibility with experience and service range.
Design Guidelines
- Colors: Industrial tones (slate gray, deep steel blue #1A365D, industrial safety orange #F97316 highlights, vibrant WhatsApp green #25D366 for CTAs).
- Typography: Bold industrial sans-serif (e.g. Barlow, Montserrat, or Inter bold) for headlines, clean readable body text.
- Layout: Modern conversion-driven responsive landing page layout with clear visual hierarchy, pricing matrix, and photo showcases.
