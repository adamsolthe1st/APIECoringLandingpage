Basis of Pricing

Standard scope. Rates are per hole for standard vertical or horizontal diamond coring, with normal access and concrete thickness up to 150 mm.
Additional charges. Thicker concrete, overhead or restricted-access work, heavy reinforcement, long-distance travel, reinforcement scanning, night work, and making good are quoted separately.
Quotation confirmation. Please provide the site location, hole diameter, concrete thickness, number of holes, access details, and clear site photos. Final rates are confirmed after review.
